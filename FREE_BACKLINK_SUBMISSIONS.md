### Post Body Template:
```text
Hey everyone! 👋

Like many of you, I frequently need to merge bank statements, sign documents, or compress PDF files. But whenever I used mainstream online tools, I encountered:
1. Annoying daily 2-file limits or subscription popups.
2. Privacy concerns regarding where my confidential documents are uploaded.

So I built iCreatePDF (https://icreatepdf.online) — a completely free, client-side PDF engineering suite.

Key highlights:
- 🔒 100% Private: All processing happens in-memory on your device using WebAssembly & Canvas. Zero files are sent to a server (you can even use it in Airplane mode).
- 🚀 No Limits: No file size caps, no daily quotas, and no account required.
- ✨ 70+ Tools: Merge, split, compress, edit, sign, OCR, and convert between PDF, Word, Excel, and JPG.

I'd love your feedback, feature ideas, or bug reports!
```
